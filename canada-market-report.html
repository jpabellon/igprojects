Why a Reliable Churn Rate Cannot Be Calculated

Zoho CRM does not systematically track churn events. Key issues:

  • ~57% of Canadian customer accounts (287 of 500) have a null Subscription_Status — these could be active, lapsed, or historical with no way to distinguish.
  • No "Former Customer" or "Churned" Account_Type values exist anywhere in the system.
  • Only 10 accounts globally have a Cancelled or Expired status — clearly an under-count of actual churn events.
  • Closed Lost deals (119 total, ~4 Canadian) are a partial signal for prospects who never converted, not existing customer churn.
Recommendation: Reliable churn metrics require either (a) Recurly cancellation event data, or (b) a systematic CRM process to update Subscription_Status when accounts cancel. Currently, CRM churn data is not actionable.
